How to Answer the Reflection Questions

Use these steps to answer the three reflection questions at the end of Unrest in the Colonies. Each answer should be two or three full sentences with a reason and a piece of evidence.

Question 1 Question 2 Question 3 Back to the Activity

Before You Start

  1. Go to unrest.mrbsocialstudies.org and sign in with your first name, last initial, and period.
  2. Using a different Chromebook than before? Click Load a backup first.
  3. Scroll to Reflect and turn in. It opens only after your pair has spent exactly 36 points.
  4. Have your paper tracker next to you. It shows your arrows, your ratings, and your pair’s points.
Three rules for every answer:
  • Use because, so, or led to to give a reason.
  • Name real events and real numbers. Do not write “it” or “that event.”
  • Write in direct voice. No “I think” or “I believe.”

Check and Turn In

Check every answer before you copy:

  • Each answer has two or more full sentences.
  • Each answer gives a reason with because, so, or led to.
  • Question 3 has at least one number from the class data.
  • You used at least two Unit 2 words.
  • No “I think” or “I believe.”

Turn it in:

  1. Press Copy my work.
  2. Open the Canvas assignment Unrest in the Colonies and press Start Assignment.
  3. Paste with Ctrl + V, then press Submit Assignment.
  4. Go back to the activity and check the box that says you submitted.
