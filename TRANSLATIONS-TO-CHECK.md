## Unrest scale: prompt and meanings (Oct 2026)

The English prompt and the eight meanings were rewritten so the scale measures
how angry people were and how many of them, not which action they took. The
translations in `index.html` still carry the older action-based meanings, so a
Level 0 reader in these languages sees the old key until this is done.

### English (what is live, for reference)

Prompt: *Imagine you live in the colonies. How angry would this make people, and how many of them? Pick the number that fits.*

| # | Name (unchanged) | New meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Barely noticed | Almost nobody cares. Life goes on. |
| 2 | Grumbling | A few people are annoyed and say so to friends. |
| 3 | Upset | Many people are bothered. It comes up in town and in the papers. |
| 4 | Angry | Most people are angry, and they want Britain to hear it. |
| 5 | Protest | Anger is strong and shared. People are ready to act together. |
| 6 | Fury | Crowds are furious. Tempers are hot enough to turn rough. |
| 7 | Defiance | Whole colonies are angry together and no longer willing to obey. |
| 8 | Ready to fight | Anger is everywhere, and people will risk their lives over it. |
